Privacy policy

Hermy Butler is used only by members of one household, with their own Google accounts.

It accesses Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts only to do tasks the account owner asks for. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared, except with the AI service that processes the request.

Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
